Problem 12

[4 kyu to 2 kyu level · Next move] Find the move that does two things at once

A next-move problem built from a game on Shogi Wars.

Black to move. You are playing Gangi, and White is in a Left Mino castle.

Find the next move.

Squares are written file first, then rank, as labeled on the board (33 = file 3, rank 3).

Show answer

Answer and explanation

☗P-33+

This move kills two birds with one stone: the tokin gives check, and your rook’s sideways line opens up at the same time.

It is an unusual kind of check that also attacks the rook.

When White is in a Left Mino, getting a foothold with a pawn on 34 is a big deal, and moves like this can arise.
