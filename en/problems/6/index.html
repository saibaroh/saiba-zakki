Problem 6

[3 dan and above · Tsume] How do you finish it off? An idea from a practical position

I tried Shogi Wars' Tsume Battle mode soon after it was introduced. I know mating practice matters, but solving problems on my own can feel like routine training compared with playing an opponent, so I appreciated a way to make it more enjoyable.

It is encouraging to see a game with so many active players try new ways to play shogi, including Sprint mode. As a shogi fan, I hope more people find a form of the game they enjoy.

Many of the problems felt like basic kyu-level practice, but this one left me stuck. It looks as though moving the golds closer should work, yet it needs an extra idea. The answer made me stop and think, “So that was it!”

Show answer

Answer

After G62 → K81, the key is L*88!

If the horse captures the lance, or a piece interposes and blocks the dragon's vertical line, a simple gold move gives mate. Against S83, P82 leads to mate. If the dragon captures the lance, the rook recaptures and mate follows.

The silver interposition on 83

This is the line shown in the original article's analysis screenshot:

G62 → K81 → L88 → S83 → P*82 → K92 → Gx83, mate.

The knights in hand are not needed. The hard part for me was noticing that the rook on 58 could help deliver mate.

In practical mating problems, I tend to dismiss pieces that seem irrelevant. It is enjoyable when an unexpected piece turns out to matter.
