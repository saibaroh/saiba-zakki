## Answer and explanation

**Yes, there is a mate (mate in 5).**

**☗B*33 → ☖K-31 → ☗G*22 → ☖K-41 → ☗N-53 (no promotion)**

White’s replies here are just one example. Other replies are also mated.

At first glance, it may look like you are a little short of pieces.

The key, however, is that the final ☗N-53, without promoting, works.
