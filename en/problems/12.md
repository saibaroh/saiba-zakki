## Answer and explanation

**☗P-33+**

```board

moves: 3d3c+
caption: Answer
```

This move kills two birds with one stone: the tokin gives check, and your rook’s sideways line opens up at the same time.

It is an unusual kind of check that also attacks the rook.

When White is in a Left Mino, getting a foothold with a pawn on 34 is a big deal, and moves like this can arise.
