Problem 14

[10 kyu to 5 kyu level · Next move] Find the chance to use your bishop

Black to move.

Find the next move.

Squares are written file first, then rank, as labeled on the board (33 = file 3, rank 3).

Show hint

You have a bishop in hand, and White’s king can be checked by a bishop right away. Look for a chance to use your bishop.

Show answer

Answer and explanation

☗B*36

This gives check and attacks the promoted silver on 47 at the same time.

If White then moves the king out of check, for example with ☖K-82, take the promoted silver with ☗Bx47. You gain material and remove White’s attacking piece, and Black is winning comfortably.

Another try: ☗P-65

At first glance, ☗P-65 also looks like a good move: it attacks the rook and opens your bishop’s diagonal.

But if White moves the rook away with ☖R-35, the rook’s line covers 36 and stops the correct ☗B*36. You miss your chance, so be careful.
