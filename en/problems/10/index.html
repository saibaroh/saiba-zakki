Problem 10

[10 kyu to 5 kyu level · Tsume] Mate in 5 with a common tesuji

Black to move. Find the mate in 5.

It may be a little tricky if you aren’t used to it, but give it your best shot!

Squares are written file first, then rank, as labeled on the board (72 = file 7, rank 2).

Show answer

Answer and explanation

☗Sx82+ → ☖Kx82 → ☗B71 → ☖Kx71 → ☗G72

The key is whether you can find the tesuji of sacrificing the bishop to lure the king into a head-gold mate (a gold dropped directly in front of the king).

If White answers ☗B71 with ☖K-92 instead, ☗G82 is still mate.

This tesuji comes up very often, so be sure to learn it!

My sense is that once you can solve this one, you are at an intermediate level.
