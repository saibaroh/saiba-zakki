Problem 9

[4 kyu to 2 kyu level · Mate or not?] Is there a forced mate in this position?

Black to move. Is there a forced mate against White’s king in this position?

If you think there is, try to find the mating sequence too.

Squares are written file first, then rank, as labeled on the board (33 = file 3, rank 3).

Show answer

Answer and explanation

Yes, there is a mate (mate in 5).

☗B33 → ☖K-31 → ☗G22 → ☖K-41 → ☗N-53 (no promotion)

White’s replies here are just one example. Other replies are also mated.

At first glance, it may look like you are a little short of pieces.

The key, however, is that the final ☗N-53, without promoting, works.
