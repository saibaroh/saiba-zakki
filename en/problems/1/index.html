Problem 1

Your Bishop Is Under Attack — Can You Finish the Game Right Here?

This position comes from one of my own games on Shogi Wars, edited to make it a cleaner problem with a single correct answer. It is aimed at kyu players: find the flashy move that settles the game.

Black to move. The bishop on 66 is under attack from White's silver.

Squares are written file first, then rank, as labeled on the board (34 = file 3, rank 4).

Hint: Find the move that decides the game right here.

Show answer

Answer and explanation

☗Rx34 → ☖Px34 → ☗Bx22+

Give up the rook to take the gold. After White recaptures with the pawn, the bishop takes the silver on 22 and promotes.

You lose the rook, but you win both a gold and a silver, and you are pressing hard on White's king. After the bishop promotes, there is also a threat of mate with ☗G*32, and White cannot hold the position together.

Black now has a decisive advantage.

A bad example: moving the bishop away

If you move the bishop away because it is under attack, White takes your knight with ☖Gx25.

☗B-88 → ☖Gx25

This misses the chance, and the game quickly turns against you.

This move may have been hard to spot at first glance. Here, White's pawn moving away let your bishop reach the silver.

Remember that when a piece moves, it can open up a chance to capture one of your opponent's pieces!
