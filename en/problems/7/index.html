Problem 7

[1 kyu to 2 dan level · Next move] Your knight’s head is under attack — what now?

In the original game this position came up with White to move; the sides have been swapped here so that you play Black.

Black to move. White is attacking the head of your knight on 77. Think of a three-move sequence.

Squares are written file first, then rank, as labeled on the board (77 = file 7, rank 7).

Show hint

Even if your knight gets taken, look for a way to win material back.

Show answer

Answer and explanation

☗N-65 → ☖Nx65 → ☗S-66

When you jump with ☗N-65, White captures your knight with ☖Nx65.

However, by drawing White’s knight out to 65, you can win it back with ☗S-66.

A bad example: other moves

With any other move, White attacks your knight’s head and you simply lose material.

When the head of your knight is under attack, consider jumping the knight, drawing in your opponent’s piece, and then winning it back!
